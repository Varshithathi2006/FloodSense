Damage Information Extractor Engine

Mathematical formula, Ground Sampling Distance (GSD), and structural metric derivation details.

Ground Sampling Distance (GSD) Physical Area Formula

$$\text{GSD} = \frac{2 \times \text{Altitude}(30\text{m}) \times \tan(\text{FOV}/2)}{\text{Image Width}}$$

$$\text{Physical Area}(m^2) = \text{Pixel Count} \times \text{GSD}^2$$

Converts raw aerial mask pixel counts into verifiable real-world square meters ($m^2$) using nadir drone camera geometry.

Weighted Incident Severity Index Formula

$$\text{Score} = \frac{\sum_{c=0}^{9} W_c \times \text{Coverage}_c \%}{\sum W_c \times 100} \times 100$$

Weights: Flooded Building ($W=10$), Flooded Road ($W=8$), Water ($W=5$), Pool ($W=3$), Vehicle ($W=2$). Tiers: Critical ($\ge 15$), Severe ($8-15$), Moderate ($3-8$), Minor ($0.5-3$).
