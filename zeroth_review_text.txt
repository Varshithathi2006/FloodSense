INTRODUCTION & MOTIVATION
One of the most destructive natural calamities, floods seriously damage people's lives,
property, and the environment.
Manual inspection doesn't scale during large-scale disasters, but quick and accurate flood
assessment is essential for efficient disaster response and resource allocation.
There is a gap between detection and reliable, actionable insight since current AI methods
either identify flood water without generating useful reports or generate reports without
verifiable footing in actual facts.
FloodSense creates an automatic flood assessment report from the input image. It then uses
RAG and an LLM to cross-check past flood cases and conventional response protocols to
recommend reaction steps.

R E S E A R C H  G A P
Lack of robust multi-class flood segmentation: The majority of flood detection techniques currently in use
perform binary water/no-water segmentation using a single technique without benchmarking across multiple
approaches (classical, foundation models like SAM, transformer-based segmenters) or differentiating
between flood water, debris, and damaged structures. This results in coarse, unreliable masks that don't
capture the full picture required for damage assessment.
Lack of proper report generation: Current automated systems either use standalone LLMs to generate
situational reports without connecting claims to verifiable image-derived features or authoritative sources, or
they output raw segmentation masks without any interpretation. This results in reports that are either
unusable by responders or prone to hallucinated, untraceable severity/impact assessments.
8

=== PAGE 9 ===
“Current flood assessment systems are inadequate in two ways: text-based systems generate
reports from secondary web sources without direct visual grounding, making them slow,
incomplete, and unverifiable against actual site imagery, especially early in a disaster;
segmentation-only models identify flooded regions but leave severity interpretation and
reporting to humans. At the moment, no solution combines report production with fine-
grained flood segmentation so that each claim may be linked to visual proof. ”
Scope: FloodSense is an end-to-end demo dashboard that includes automated flood-water segmentation from photos
(benchmarking five CV approaches), RAG-based historical and protocol data retrieval and structured severity reports
created by LLM.
Boundaries: The project relies on existing imagery and knowledge-base documents rather than live field data collection.
It does not include live drone data ingestion, or generalization to other disaster types beyond a proof-of-concept.
